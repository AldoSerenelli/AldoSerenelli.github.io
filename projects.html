LUNANOVA

An ERC Synergy project connecting precision nuclear measurements with the next generation of solar and solar-like stellar models. The project started on 1 September 2026.

02

ChETEC-INFRA

A European project connecting nuclear laboratories, telescopes and supercomputing facilities to study the origin of the chemical elements. I was Principal Investigator for CSIC; the B23 solar-model resources were developed within this framework.

EU Horizon 2020 · 2021–2025 · Grant 101008324
Project website ↗Solar-model data →
03

4MOST — S4 / 4MIDABLE-HR

The high-resolution survey of the Milky Way disc and bulge uses stellar chemistry and motions to investigate our Galaxy’s formation and evolution. I lead its asteroseismic subsurveys, connecting spectroscopy with stellar masses and ages.

S4 survey website ↗Galactic research →
04

PLATO

Stellar modelling supports the interpretation of asteroseismic observations and the determination of stellar properties for exoplanet studies. I led the Stellar Models work package within the PLATO Science Management Consortium until 2024, and continue to be part of the Consortium.
