The B23 generation

The B23 standard solar models incorporate nuclear reaction inputs from Solar Fusion III and solar-composition choices including MB22 and AAG21. The complete archive additionally includes models using GS98, AGSS09 and C11 compositions; MB22 is provided in photospheric and meteoritic variants.

These resources include radial structure and abundance profiles, solar-neutrino fluxes and correlations, response tables and tools for exploring variations in model inputs.

The models were computed by Yago Herrera within the ChETEC-INFRA project, grant agreement 101008324, with Aldo Serenelli.

Individual files

Structure files include helioseismic quantities, individual neutrino-production distributions and chemical profiles. Flux files contain integrated predictions and the associated uncertainty/correlation information.

Abundance uncertainties and the zero point

For meteoritic mixtures, the uncertainty budget includes a common normalization (“zero-point”) uncertainty linking meteoritic refractory abundances to the photospheric scale, anchored to silicon. This contribution is correlated across refractory elements and is additional to their individual statistical uncertainties. The zero-point and statistical contributions are combined in quadrature.

The meteoritic flux-file header labels this contribution as “incl. zero point”; the entirely photospheric mixtures are labelled “no zero point”.

Provenance and version

The archive is a copy of SolarModels_ICE2023_v1.2.zip from the linked Zenodo record. Model files, README and tools retain their original contents.

The individual model files offered on this page have not been verified as identical to those within the Zenodo archive. Consult the documentation accompanying the files you use.

Solar calibration

The models were calibrated using L☉ = 3.8275 × 10³³ erg s⁻¹ and R☉ = 6.9599 × 10¹⁰ cm.
